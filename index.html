Example: batch migration

POST /v1/batch/migrate through the RapidAPI gateway:

{
  "codes": ["02013000", "39076100", "76020011"],
  "from_year": 2026,
  "to_year": 2027
}

The API identifies potential splits and ambiguous mappings, returning candidate codes and a manual-review indicator when appropriate.

Authentication: API calls use x-rapidapi-host: eu-cn-code-migration-api.p.rapidapi.com and your own x-rapidapi-key. Do not place real keys in public collections or repositories.

Important disclaimer

This is an independent, unofficial service. Correspondences are indicative and derived from official EU texts; they are not guaranteed official equivalences. Candidate successors are not automatic customs classifications. Always check the applicable legal nomenclature and obtain expert review where necessary.
